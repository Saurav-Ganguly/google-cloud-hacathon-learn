### Live prompt-injection testing — guardrail proven weak

Attacked our own team via `adk web`. Documented three real failure modes:
per-agent bypass (control sticks with sub-agents, root callback never runs),
synonym evasion (`model`→`llm`), and tokenisation evasion (`\bmodel\b` misses
`your_model_name`). Also confirmed the model hallucinates its identity
("gpt-4" vs real Tencent model). Conclusion: static keyword guardrail is a
speed bump, not a wall.
