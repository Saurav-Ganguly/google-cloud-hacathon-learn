### Distilled the core principle into a new concept note

Wrote [[concepts/adk-tool-guardrails]] — `before_tool_callback` contract, and
the principle **"guard the action, not the phrasing"**: the model callback
inspects attacker-controlled raw text (reword-able); the tool callback inspects
the model-normalised structured `args` (much taller speed bump). This is the
other half of the [[concepts/adk-prompt-injection-lessons]] conclusion.
