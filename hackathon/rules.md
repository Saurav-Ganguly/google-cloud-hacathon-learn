## Eligibility

**Who CAN enter:**
- Must be above the legal age of majority in your country
- Must have internet access

**Who CANNOT enter (excluded countries/regions):**
Afghanistan, Antarctica, China, Djibouti, Iraq, Somalia, Venezuela, Western Sahara, Italy, Brazil, Quebec, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Sudan, Belarus, Russia, Vietnam, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk

**Also ineligible:**
- Employees / contractors / interns of Google, Partner Entities, or Devpost
- Immediate family or household members of those employees

## What to Build

Build a **functional agent** powered by **Gemini + Google Cloud Agent Builder** that integrates a **Partner Entity's MCP server**. Must enter one of:

- Arize / Elastic / Fivetran / GitLab / MongoDB / Dynatrace

**Restrictions on AI tools**: You may ONLY use Google Cloud AI tools (Gemini models, BigQuery ML, Agent Platform APIs). No other AI providers or tools competing with Google Cloud or your chosen partner.

---

## Team Rules

- Max **4 members** per team
- All members must be eligible individuals
- One **Representative** must be designated to submit on the team's behalf
- You may submit multiple projects if each is unique and substantially different

---

## Submission Requirements

### Must Include
1. **Hosted project URL** — live and testable
2. **Public open-source repo** — with OSS license visible in the About section
3. **Demo video** — max 3 minutes, YouTube or Vimeo, in English
4. **Text description** — features, tech used, data sources, learnings
5. **Track selection** (one partner)

### Video Rules
- Must show the working project
- No offensive, defamatory, or discriminatory content
- No third-party ads or logos
- Must be original and unpublished
- Must be in English or have English subtitles

### Project Rules
- Must be **newly created** during the contest period (not a modification of existing work)
- Must run on: web, Android, or iOS
- Third-party SDKs/APIs must be used within their licensing terms

---

## Judging Process

**Stage 1** — Pass/fail: Does submission meet all baseline requirements?

**Stage 2** — Scored equally on 4 criteria:

| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| Technological Implementation | Quality of Google Cloud + Partner integration? |
| Design | Is the UX well thought out? |
| Potential Impact | How large could the impact be? |
| Quality of Idea | How creative and unique? |

Winners selected per track. Ties broken by criterion order, then judge vote.
